Types of ASNB Funds (Overview)

ASNB funds are generally split into Fixed-Price funds (price locked at RM 1.00/unit) and Variable-Price funds (price fluctuates based on Net Asset Value / NAV).

Fund Category Examples Price Structure Risk Profile
Fixed-Price Funds ASB, ASB 2, ASB 3 Didik, ASM, ASM 2 Wawasan, ASM 3 Fixed @ RM 1.00 Low Risk / Capital Guaranteed
Variable-Price Funds ASN Equity 2, ASN Imbang, ASN Sara, ASN Global Floating (NAV based) Medium to High Risk / Growth potential
